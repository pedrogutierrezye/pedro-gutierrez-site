About

Corporate discipline, founder's instinct

I'm a Paraguayan-born finance executive based in Germany. My work sits where finance meets change: transforming how finance runs, steering performance with clear numbers, and building new businesses from the first business case onwards.

At REPA (Parts Town Europe) I lead finance transformation across 10 European legal entities, including OneStream consolidation, management reporting and a group-wide Order-to-Cash program that improved DSO by 7% year over year. Before that I planned and forecast a USD 14B portfolio at Microsoft, and held full P&L responsibility for a USD 52M digital business at Ciao GmbH, a Microsoft company.

Alongside my corporate career I build companies. In 2013 I founded Polaris, my first company, then co-founded Prestamena and Infinita and helped Negociable come to life. That is when I realised that developing businesses was what I did best, and I gave it a name: inkubeitor. I work in Spanish, English and German.
